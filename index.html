Associate Professor | University of Colorado, Colorado Springs (UCCS)

  • Research Lab: LiCA (Lab for intelligent Computer Architecture)

Research Interests

Computer architecture, AI acceleration - hardware and software, deep-learning-based intelligent computing (AI computing), application-specific embedded systems (mobile processors), diffusion applications (characterization, acceleration), data augmentation (MRI data generation for AD, MS), memory performance (last-level cache, TLB, HBM), workload characterization of emerging applications, data analysis, parallel computing and parallel architecture design, performance modeling, low power design, early-stage power estimation, and FPGA prototyping.
